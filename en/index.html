A route that avoids rework

Four steps from brief to delivery; each one has a checkpoint.

  1. 01Define the deliverableLength, use, format, loudness and licence, written down first.
  2. 02Pick the toolChoose by the result you need, not by model hype.
  3. 03Put control into the promptStructure, tempo, instrumentation and forbidden sounds, named one by one.
  4. 04Listen again before deliveryChange systems, compare with a reference, check loudness and stem names.
